# Scope and non-goals

## Initial scope
Local single-user deployment; multiple profiles/resumes; configurable searches; permitted public job-source adapters; normalization, deduplication, eligibility and explainable ranking; application preparation; revision-bound batch approval; supported browser flows with exception handling; tracking, logs, exports, backup and restore.

## Non-goals
- Guaranteed access to every job board or employer portal.
- CAPTCHA/MFA bypass, unauthorized login-wall scraping or rate-limit evasion.
- Blind submission without reviewed batch approval.
- Guaranteed 24/7 operation while the local machine is off.
- Mandatory paid AI/API, hosted database or cloud browser.
- Microservices, Kubernetes or distributed consensus before evidence.
- Multi-tenant SaaS before explicit requirements and threat model.
- ML ranking before deterministic baseline and evaluation data.

Future PostgreSQL, broker, multi-user RBAC, extra adapters or remote deployment require evidence and an ADR. Candidate remains responsible for personal facts and legal attestations.
